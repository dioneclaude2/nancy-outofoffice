What to pack

Bring your brightest.

Nancy is fun, colourful and as bright as possible — and so is this trip. Pack your cutest, most colourful summer outfits. Think tropical, think holiday, think joy.

The only rule

Colour. And then more colour.

White and beige are welcome — just give them something loud to sit next to. Pink and yellow are the house colours, so wear them if you've got them. And bling is always welcome.

Nancy pink Marigold Coral Turquoise Lime Cream

Yes please

  • Bright, saturated colour — the louder the better
  • Pink and yellow, our house colours
  • Bling: gold, pearls, crystals, stacked and shiny
  • Bikinis and swimwear — you'll live in them
  • Cute pyjamas for movie night
  • White and beige, styled with colour pops
  • Prints, florals, stripes, crochet, sequins

Maybe leave it

  • All-black outfits
  • Anything dark, moody or muted
  • Heavy fabrics — it's hot and humid
  • Head-to-toe neutral with nothing bright
  • Heels for the beach or the boat

The checklist

Four days, packed.

Pool & beach

  • Bikinis & swimsuits (bring several)
  • Kaftan or crochet cover-up
  • Straw hat & sunglasses
  • Sandals or slides
  • Reef-safe sunscreen

Boat day

  • Swimwear you can move in
  • Sarong or light cover-up
  • A hat that won't blow away
  • Flat shoes — no heels on deck
  • A change of clothes

Dinners

  • Colourful maxi or sundress
  • A co-ord set or two
  • Your best bling
  • Something for golden hour photos
  • A light layer for the evening

Movie night

  • Your cutest pyjamas
  • Bonus points for matching sets
  • Pastels, prints or silk
  • Fluffy slides or socks
  • It's going on camera — make it cute

Pack bright. Pack cute. Pack for the photos.

See you in Playa del Carmen.
